Joel Hale began life in Altadena, California. His father was a gymnast and erected bars and equipment in the backyard. He spent many years in gymnastics as a competitor, and as a high-level coach, with training in Moscow. His gymnastics career led to TV news interviews and a prime-time appearance.

While working part-time at lumberyard in Los Angeles, Joel supplemented his income by doing back flips for quarters. That led to a prime-time television appearance when he was discovered by one of the hosts of George Schlatter's Real People who came in to buy a screen door. As bachelor number two on The Dating Game, he won a week in London.

He took a brief hiatus from gymnastics to experience living off the land in the Yukon Wilderness. After a career change, he became a software engineer, contracted by the Department of Defense and other organizations. Hungering for competition, Joel discovered swing dancing and travelled internationally for competition. His daughter and granddaughter were competitive gymnasts and swing dancers also.

Additionally, Joel has worked construction and has been published in two technical gymnastics journals. Currently, he is a novelist and lives in Walnut Creek, California.
