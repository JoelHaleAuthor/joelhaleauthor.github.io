When fragments of a hidden program surface - old photographs, a cryptic code, a name whispered across continents, Alli is pulled into a conspiracy that reaches from wartime Hull to a jungle escape in 1971 and into the heart of modern-day San Francisco. As corporate power, shadowy projects, and family secrets collide, Alli must navigate shifting loyalties, impossible choices, and a threat that uses DNA and data as weapons.

Lost and Found: Legacy and Hope is a propulsive, emotionally charged thriller where every revelation raises the stakes. Love becomes leverage, history becomes evidence, and one desperate act forces a reckoning that will echo across generations. Fast, fierce, and heartbreakingly human, this is a novel about what we inherit—and what we choose to save.
JC "Jayce" Pullman doesn't remember entering the raffle he won. By the second night he's sharing whisky, movie quotes, and far too many secrets with Alli Daws: a former public defender with an easy laugh, a quiet confidence, and a father nobody can find much information about online.

Back home outside San Francisco, the easy part ends. A line of code Jayce swears he never wrote. A woman who says all the right things and never once blushes. A family that has spent fifty years quietly agreeing not to ask each other certain questions.

Alli has always known her family was a little different. She's about to find out exactly how different — and how far someone might go to make sure it stays a secret.

The fourth novel in the Lost and Found series, this is a story about the families that find us, the truths we can't outrun, and what love is worth risking when it matters most.
