SWINGING OUT is a genre-blending novel of reinvention, where a woman running from her past finds an unconventional family, a dance floor for her talents, and the nerve to choose her own path.


Samantha McClain has one suitcase, a bruised cheek, and a plan that fits in a single phrase: get to her brother in Los Angeles.

Three days later, she's knocking on what she believes is her brother's door. The woman who answers is very pregnant and not what Sam expected. Within a day, Sam has a new family, a new nickname for her ex ("Dick Witless"), and a new roommate, even more pregnant, and who radiates calm.

Sam has always been good at systems, code, and gymnastics. She has never been good at herself. Every man she dated made the decisions, and every time she told herself it wasn't that bad. Now, in a city where nobody knows her story, she decides to take control of her life.

She finds a new job and a swing dance floor that could change her life. She gets to know a family full of talent and fierce loyalty. Some of them are famous, some are dangerous, some are brilliant, and some are all three.

Sam is learning that she deserves better than what she had in Nebraska. But first she has to decide what that looks like.
